Social Media Trends in Healthcare: 2027

A short report focused on the Saudi Arabian healthcare market

Executive Summary

Social media is becoming an important part of healthcare marketing in Saudi Arabia. Patients increasingly use social platforms to discover doctors, evaluate healthcare providers, and find medical information. Based on current usage data and Saudi patient behavior, Instagram is recommended as the primary advertising platform for healthcare organizations in 2027.

Key Trends

  • Short-form video: Reels and short educational videos are becoming major formats for healthcare communication.
  • Trust and credibility: Patients value qualified doctors, reviews, and reliable medical information.
  • Digital patient journey: Social media can influence patients before they decide to book an appointment.
  • AI-assisted content: Healthcare organizations are increasingly using AI to support content creation and personalization, while medical accuracy remains essential.

Recommendation for 2027

Instagram should be the primary healthcare advertising platform in 2027. Its combination of strong Saudi reach, doctor discovery, visual communication, short-form video, and patient engagement makes it particularly suitable for healthcare organizations.

Healthcare brands should focus on educational Reels, doctor introductions, patient FAQs, medical awareness content, reviews, and clear appointment calls-to-action.

Conclusion

The healthcare social media landscape is moving toward short-form video, trusted medical experts, and personalized digital communication. Although Snapchat and TikTok offer significant reach in Saudi Arabia, current evidence indicates that Instagram provides a strong balance between audience reach, doctor discovery, trust-building, and patient conversion.
